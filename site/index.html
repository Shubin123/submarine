Finite Volume vs.
Lattice Boltzmann

A practical choice framework for vehicle-flow simulation and a fair plan for connecting validated CFD to Gazebo.

Bottom line

Begin with a finite-volume baseline for the submarine hull. Evaluate LBM on the same low-Mach, single-phase conditions only after matching error and refinement criteria. Use Gazebo for robot dynamics and control integration—not as the CFD solver.

What this contains

  • A method and risk comparison
  • OpenFOAM, OpenLB, Palabos, AeroToolbox, and Gazebo roles
  • A matched-accuracy benchmark plan and acceptance criteria
